02 / PRODUCTION PIPELINE

Brief · Engineer · Render · Polish · Deliver

A proven 4-stage pipeline that guides you from a raw client concept to a pristine, artifact-free visual asset without wasting GPU credits.

Start with strict constraints, not random prompt rolls

Never fire up a generative tool without a clear objective. Every Zero To AI project begins with a 3-line creative brief: Core Value Proposition, Visual Tone & Palette, Camera Grammar, and Delivery Aspect Ratio.

  • ✓ Establish clear commercial positioning or narrative intent
  • ✓ Lock delivery aspect ratios: 9:16 (Vertical Reels) or 16:9 (Cinematic Commercial)
  • ✓ Prevent wasted generation credits on aimless iterations
commercial-brief.md
# BRIEF 01: ISOTONIC BEVERAGE SPOT - Objective: 10s high-octane commercial, condensation beads on aluminum can - Lighting: Studio neon cyber-blue rim lighting, deep contrast shadows - Motion: 360-degree slow-motion rotation + explosive water droplets - Output: 16:9 4K Master & 9:16 Short-Form Social Cut

Prompt Architecture: Subject + Lighting + Camera Physics

Discard meaningless buzzwords like "hyperrealistic 8k". Master syntactic prompt engineering with explicit focal lengths, shutter speeds, sensor characteristics, and volumetric lighting tags.

  • ✓ Lock subject core identifiers for multi-shot consistency
  • ✓ Direct camera paths: Pan, Orbit, Drone descent, Anamorphic push-in
  • ✓ Deploy cinematic lighting tokens: Rim lighting, Volumetric haze, Golden hour diffusion

/imagine prompt: cinematic commercial hero shot of metallic beverage can with tactile condensation beads, electric blue neon rim light, volumetric atmospheric haze, shot on 35mm anamorphic prime lens, shallow depth of field, f/1.4 --ar 16:9 --v 6.1 --style raw

Multi-Model Render Pipeline & Temporal Consistency

Orchestrate best-in-class tools: Flux.1 & Midjourney for razor-sharp initial keyframes, paired with Runway Gen-3 Alpha & Kling AI for fluid, coherent camera motion and natural physics.

  • ✓ Preserve structural fidelity without facial distortion or hand warping
  • ✓ Map motion velocity to sound design tempo and pacing beats
  • ✓ AI upscale to pristine 4K resolution with micro-grain stabilization
